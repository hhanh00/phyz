For the positive/negative-frequency parts of relativistic solutions, the user prefers "term" (e.g. "the $v$-terms") or an explicit description ("the negative-energy solutions") — not "branch".

**Why:** They questioned the word when it appeared in qft.md; even though the sequence's earlier pages (relativistic-qm.md, dirac-equation.md) use "branch" and define it via the dispersion relation's two sheets, they find it informal and prefer plainer labels.

**How to apply:** When writing or editing the phyz docs, default to "term"/explicit description. If earlier pages using "branch" are quoted or linked, leave their text alone unless asked to sweep them. See [[phyz-prose-style]] for related prose preferences.
